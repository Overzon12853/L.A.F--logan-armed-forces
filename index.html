WHO ARE WE?

Logan Armed Forces — L.A.F is an active Roblox gaming community built around military gaming, teamwork, organization, diplomacy and community interaction.

Our members participate in military games, organized activities, events, training, operations and community projects.

Politics and diplomacy also play an important secondary role within L.A.F, allowing our members to experience leadership, alliances, negotiations and strategic decision-making.

But above everything else, L.A.F exists to build an active community where people can play, communicate, improve and enjoy their time together.

WHAT WE DO

MILITARY GAMING

Participate in Roblox military games, organized operations, training sessions and community activities.

DAILY ACTIVITY

L.A.F operates on a regular basis with members, staff and events helping keep the community alive and active.

DIPLOMACY

Interact with other communities, form alliances, negotiate agreements and experience organized diplomacy.

POLITICS

Take part in the political and leadership side of the community and develop your ability to organize, communicate and lead.

COMMUNITY

Meet new people, make friends and become part of a group that values activity and teamwork.

SUPPORT

L.A.F believes strong communities help their members. New and inexperienced players should have opportunities to learn, improve and become stronger.
